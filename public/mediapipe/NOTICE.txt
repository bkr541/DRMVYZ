wasm/vision_wasm_internal.js and wasm/vision_wasm_internal.wasm come from the
@mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.0 npm package (wasm/ folder).
face_landmarker.task is Google's "Face Landmarker" model (float16, v1):
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task

Used by the Headliner engine's Face Warp and Face Echo effects, which load them
from /mediapipe/ so face tracking works offline. The SIMD-less runtime
(vision_wasm_nosimd_internal.*) is deliberately not shipped: Electron's Chromium
always supports WebAssembly SIMD.

selfie_segmenter.tflite is Google's "Selfie Segmenter" model (float16):
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_segmenter/float16/latest/selfie_segmenter.tflite
Used by the Headliner silhouette effects (Aura, Energy Outline, Outline Echo, Particle Body).

pose_landmarker_lite.task is Google's "Pose Landmarker (lite)" model (float16, v1):
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/pose_landmarker/pose_landmarker_lite/float16/1/pose_landmarker_lite.task
Used by the Headliner engine's Spark Skeleton effect.
