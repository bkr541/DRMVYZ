# Layout Lab preset cards

The second stable tab (`layout-lab-tab-02`), named **Preset cards** by default,
contains five rows: Cinema poster, Framed print, Gallery tile, Compact strip and
Studio monitor. Renaming the tab preserves its content. Existing saved tab names
are respected.

Each row compares Reactor, Afterhours, CANOPY and PSYLO from Cinema 2.0's preset
browser. Images are the original full-color production-renderer frames in
`public/preset-artwork/`, generated by `scripts/generate-preset-artwork.mjs`.
Every treatment preserves the complete 16:9 frame without blueprint filters,
color effects or cropping. The poster adds a small caption scrim at the bottom.

The rows share the application's neutral surfaces, cyan selection/focus accents,
6px card corners and canonical typography roles. Selection and favorites are
independent per row and remain local to the study. No engine, audio or cast
runtime is started. Narrow panels scroll each row horizontally.
