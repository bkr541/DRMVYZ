# Cinema 2.0 Starters preset

## Approved visual baseline

The supplied 1326 × 1187 Starters illustration is the visual reference. The native fullscreen module reconstructs the scene procedurally in 3D; the reference is not bundled or sampled by the renderer. The following characteristics are mandatory:

- a closed orange-brown field case viewed from above and toward its right side;
- two dark straps, a pale lid label, two front latches, and a centered handle;
- three rounded red-and-white capture balls in the exact reference arrangement;
- illustrated grass, foliage, cast shadows, highlights, and the dark forest background;
- the reference camera perspective, silhouettes, proportions, palette, and lighting.

The procedural renderer uses signed-distance geometry, a perspective camera, scene lighting, contact shadows, material shading, and generated grass detail. The authored **Case Open** baseline is `0`, matching the approved closed case. Manual geometry and palette controls operate on the generated scene rather than a flattened image.
