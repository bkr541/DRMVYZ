# CONDUIT step 6 — floor, grade, framing, and review

This is the final implementation pass from [the production-match plan](cinema2-conduit-production-match-plan.md). The approved wordmark shape, material hierarchy, tube connections, and LED shader are unchanged.

## Changes

- Replaced the floor's broad beige base/sky colors with a neutral metallic midtone. Reduced its diffuse response to the room key light while retaining soft screen-space reflections and localized warm specular from the existing Energy Color lights.
- Increased floor roughness and restrained high-quality reflectivity so the floor no longer presents a noisy duplicate of the wordmark. Added optional low/medium reflectivity values to the shared floor effect; other presets retain their existing behavior. CONDUIT uses still lower values on those tiers because their shorter reflection marches otherwise produce chunky reflected letters.
- Raised the HDR bloom threshold and tightened its strength/spread; slightly reduced haze. The lit bars can peak without merging nearby components or softening the letter faces.
- Kept filmic tone mapping and exposure unchanged. Used a nearly neutral finish with slightly less saturation, contrast, vignette, grain, and chromatic fringing to retain bevel and metal detail.
- Kept the existing camera/framing: the locked 16:9 view already centers the full wordmark and four tubes, while `minAspect` widens the scene vertically for the narrower Stage. No camera adjustment was needed after checking both views.

These captures should receive the requester's direct sign-off before calling the preset production-ready; the floor ring design and wall architecture are still the authored 3D assets rather than a literal reconstruction of every contour in the image.
